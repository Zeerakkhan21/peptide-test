What is GLP-1?

Understanding GLP-1

GLP-1 is a hormone your body produces. It is released in the gut after eating and takes part in appetite signalling, digestion and blood sugar regulation.

It is discussed widely at the moment, and much of what circulates online is oversimplified. This page is our attempt at a clear explanation.

A role in appetite signalling

One of the signals communicating fullness between the gut and the brain.

A role in digestion

Takes part in regulating how quickly the stomach empties.

A role in blood sugar

Involved in the body's insulin response after eating.

Part of a bigger picture

Sleep, diet, movement and stress all shape metabolic health too.
